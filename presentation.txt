1. business pov Imagine we are a travel agency helping our customers find the right accomodation for their trips. In our case, we focus on Muslim travelers planning their journey to Makkah — what we call the Umrah trip. Our role is to guide them in choosing the right hotel — not just based on current prices, but across different time options. To do that, we need to provide clear visibility on both price and availability, so they can decide when would be the best time for them to travel.

2. problem Hotel prices in Makkah fluctuates significantly and can change drastically every day. Planning in advance is, most of the time, challenging— customers either miss better deals or end up paying more than necessary. At the same time, as a travel agency, we risk ourselves to failing to profit from the packages we offer, if we lack the insights from our data.

3. approach Our solution is to create a data pipeline that fetches data from hotel website8s), gather the data, transform, organize, and serve it as a dashboard. The data should be fetched daily to reduce the risk of missing out optimal offers and availability. The dashboard should help customer/user in the decision making. With the dashboard, user can: Compare average prices per night across hotel classes (3, 4, 5 stars) Identify optimal booking horizons (weeks or months in advance) Get an overview of hotel availability trends This helps user plan their trip more efficiently and cost-effectively.

4. Value for the Travel Agency For customers: Make informed decisions based on real data Find better timing for booking Avoid overpaying For us travel agency: Reduce manual research Improve pricing strategy Deliver better, data-driven recommendations Example Insights: Last-minute booking for 4-star hotels can sometimes be cheaper than 3-star-hotels due to demand pattern Booking 1 month or more in advance can reveal 5-star options at prices close to 3-star hotels Etc.

5. pipelines workflow Think of our system as a smart assistant working in the background. Every day, it collects hotel prices and availabilty — just like someone checking multiple booking websites. It then cleans and organizes the data into structured information. After that, it analyzes patterns to identify trends in pricing and availability. Finally, it presents only the key insights in a simple dashboard — so customers don‘t have to do the research themselves. So instead of guessing, they can make informed decisions based on real data.
