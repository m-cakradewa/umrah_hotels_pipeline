The Mission

We act as a specialized travel agency for Muslim travelers, focusing on the spiritual journey of Umrah to Makkah. Our goal is to simplify the complex accommodation landscape.

The Strategy

We move beyond static "today's price" offers. By providing visibility on price trends and availability across different time windows, we empower travelers to choose the optimal travel dates.

The Challenge

Volatile Prices & Planning Gaps

Makkah's hospitality market is unpredictable, creating a lose-lose situation for uninformed travelers and agencies.

Price Variance: +200%

Extreme Price Fluctuations

Hotel prices in Makkah change drastically every day based on Islamic calendar events and demand. What costs $100 today might cost $500 next week.

Customer Planning Struggles

Without visibility, travelers either miss the best booking windows or end up overpaying significantly. Advance planning becomes a gamble rather than a strategy.

Agency Profitability Risk

As a travel agency, blind spots in data mean we fail to optimize package pricing. We risk lower margins or unsold inventory by not anticipating market shifts.
